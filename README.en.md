# Syvo

**🌐 Language:** [Português](https://github.com/kauavfialho/syvo#readme) | **English**

A desktop app to organize your monthly money, your assets and your Brazilian income tax. It runs on Mac and Windows, with no login and no server: your data stays in a file on your own computer.

The goal is to help anyone, from a child to a grandparent, take care of their money easily and for free. The app was generated by artificial intelligence, based on an idea by Kauã Fialho.

> The app is currently available in Portuguese only, and it is designed around Brazil, including its income tax and investment rules.

## Updates

The app checks GitHub when it opens and every 6 hours. When a new version is available, it lets you know and shows the **Atualizar agora** (Update now) button: it downloads and verifies the file, makes a backup, replaces the app and reopens. You can also check with **Buscar atualização** (Check for updates) in the menu.

On Mac, Syvo must be in the Applications folder.

## Your data and backups

Your database is a file named `syvo.db`. To find it, go to **Dados e backup** (Data and backup) and click **Mostrar arquivo do banco** (Show database file). Uninstalling the app does not delete this file.

- **Automatic backup:** one copy per day when the app opens, keeping the 30 most recent.
- **Formatting or changing computers:** first use **Exportar backup** (Export backup) and save the file to a USB drive or external disk. Then install the app and use **Restaurar um backup** (Restore a backup).

The same backup works on Mac and Windows. Data does not sync on its own: to move it to another computer, restore the backup.

## Goals and learning

- **Goals:** choose how much you want to save per month and per year. The home screen shows how much is left over and how much is still missing.
- **Learn:** 100 tips to organize, save, cut expenses, build savings and deal with debt. The tips are educational and are not investment advice.
- **Learn to invest:** explains, in plain language, risk, time horizon and each type of Brazilian investment, plus the warning signs of scams. It is an educational guide and does not recommend products.
- **Text size:** in **Configurações** (Settings), choose normal, large or extra large.

## Brazilian income tax

This tab brings together each year's income, payments, dependents, assets, debts and receipts. It compares the simplified and full tax returns, points out what to review and creates a PDF report to help you fill in the official Receita Federal program.

- The result is an estimate, and the app does not file your return.
- Capital gains, stock market transactions, rural activity and the minimum tax on high incomes are not calculated.
- Each year's rules are in `app/js/ir-calculo.js`. Available years: 2025 and 2026.
- The filing requirement thresholds for the 2027 return still need to be added.
